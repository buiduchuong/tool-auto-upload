LUU Y MULTI-ACCOUNT
- Neu len VPS moi, nen tao/dang nhap lai account truc tiep tren VPS.
- Moi account can profile_dir va debug_port rieng.
- Mac dinh account moi duoc tao trong accounts/<platform>/<account_id>/chrome-profile.

YEU CAU VPS
- Windows VPS.
- Cai Google Chrome.
- Cai Python 3 va tick Add Python to PATH.
- Chay setup.bat lan dau.
- Chay run_web_panel.bat, mo http://127.0.0.1:8080 tren VPS.
- Neu muon truy cap tu may khac: dat WEB_PANEL_HOST=0.0.0.0 va mo firewall/reverse proxy co mat khau.
