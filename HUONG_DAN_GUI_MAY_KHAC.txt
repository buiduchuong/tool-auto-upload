HUONG DAN DUNG TOOL TREN MAY KHAC

1. GIAI NEN FILE ZIP
- Giai nen toan bo file zip ra mot thu muc bat ky.
- Khong doi ten rieng tung file ben trong. Nen giu nguyen ca thu muc.

2. MAY MOI CAN CO
- Windows.
- Google Chrome.
- Python 3 da cai va go duoc lenh: python
- Mang internet.

3. CAI THU VIEN LAN DAU
- Chay file: setup.bat
- Doi den khi cua so bao cai xong.

4. DANG NHAP YOUTUBE
- Chay file: run_login.bat
- Dang nhap YouTube/YouTube Studio trong Chrome vua mo.
- Sau khi dang nhap xong, giu Chrome do neu tool yeu cau.

5. MO PANEL
- Chay file: run_panel.bat
- Tab "Tai video": dan link moi dong mot link, bam "Tai video".
- Mac dinh tool tai video co am thanh, kieu giong:
  yt-dlp.exe --yes-playlist link_video
- Video tai ve nam trong thu muc: videos

6. UPLOAD VIDEO
- De video can upload vao thu muc: videos
- Sua mo ta trong panel hoac file: default_description.txt
- Bam "Upload tat ca video" hoac chon 1 video roi bam "Upload video dang chon".

7. LUU Y KHI GUI SANG MAY KHAC
- Thu muc chrome-profile khong duoc dong goi kem vi day la du lieu dang nhap cua may cu.
- Sang may moi can dang nhap lai bang run_login.bat.
- Neu tai video Facebook rieng tu, co the can cookies. Nhap them vao o "Lenh them", vi du:
  --cookies cookies.txt
- Neu may moi khong nhan lenh python, hay cai Python va tick "Add Python to PATH".
