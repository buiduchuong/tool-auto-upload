HUONG DAN DANG VIDEO QUA ZERNIO

1. Dang nhap https://zernio.com va tao Profile.
2. Ket noi Facebook/Instagram/TikTok/YouTube vao Profile.
3. Vao Settings > API Keys, tao key va copy ngay khi Zernio hien thi.
4. Mo panel, vao tab Zernio, dan API key va chon Platforms.
   Neu dung nhieu tai khoan Zernio free, dan nhieu key cach nhau bang dau phay.
5. De trong Account IDs va Profile ID neu muon dang len tat ca account da ket noi cua cac platform da chon.
6. Chon video hoac bam Dang tat ca qua Zernio (lich random).

Tool dung default_description.txt lam mo ta. Tieu de mac dinh la ten file.
Neu da co URL video public truc tiep, co the dien vao zernio_media_urls.txt de bo qua buoc upload.
TikTok duoc dat quyen rieng tu PUBLIC_TO_EVERYONE va bat cac co consent bat buoc.
Video dai hon 90 giay se tu tao ban Instagram 90 giay; Facebook/YouTube van dung video day du.
Video dai hon 10 phut se tu tao ban TikTok 9 phut 59 giay; YouTube/Facebook van dung video day du.
Video dai hon 3 phut se dang YouTube 2 lan: ban day du va mot ban Short 2 phut 59 giay khung doc 9:16 sau do 5 phut.
Zernio chi dang duoc len account dang o trang thai connected.
Neu nhap nhieu API key, tool se tu lay account connected trong tung key va dang rieng theo tung key.
Vi du key A co TikTok/YouTube, key B co Facebook/Instagram thi bam mot lan van co the len ca 4 nen tang.
Khong hen lich qua 7 ngay sau luc upload media; tool se dung va bao loi neu lich vuot gioi han nay.
